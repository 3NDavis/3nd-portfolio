# Overview
Circuitry City is an **A Hat In Time** level themed around a circuit board city.

It is most similar to the Nyakuza Metro DLC for A Hat In Time in terms from an aesthetic standpoint.

# Verticality Rules

I ran into some issues where vertical navigation felt forced and overly punishing. To find some solutions I played and analyzed some of my favorite levels in commercial 3D platformers that feature exploration in extreme verticality: _Mario Odyssey_ - Metro Kingdom, _Chibi Robo_, _aHiT_ - Nyakuza Metro, _Spyro_, _Mario 3D World Bowzers Fury_, and _The Pathless_.

I came up with the following rules that I think make a good vertical level.
1. The player cannot lose sight of the primary navigation beacon unless there is an immediate alternative goal. After that goal is achieved the player must be able to see the primary navigation marker.
2. The player should encounter mechanics that disrupt only running from a to b when traversing the same vertical layer (including the ground floor).
3. The player should always be able to travel downward easily if they are not on the ground floor.
4. The player should always be able to easily see at least 1 path to stay at roughly the same height, and at least 1 path to move upward. (even if they must go down to go up, using the rule below)
5. There should be different routes that send the player to destinations at varying speed but only go to a certain number of destinations proportional to their speed.
    - Like real world express trains, or airplanes
6. Each layer of verticality has ledges in between or is tiered (smaller than the previous layer) so that failing does not mean completely restarting a climb.
7. The player should be able to make it to the highest point and be rewarded as such.

I found that these rules create a level that is fun to navigate and is forgiving to the player.

  

## Applying the Rules

I revised the entire level again with these rules in mind. My primary navigation beacon is the central tower, to make it more noticeable from more places, I readjusted the positions of a few buildings and plan to make the central tower significantly taller. A lot of the buildings I had previously placed broke rule 6 (the tiered rule) in particular. I added in a lot more paths between sections (rule 4). And added a reason to be on the ground floor by adding “speed panels” (rule 2) that let the player move faster along the ground.
